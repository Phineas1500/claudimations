# Reference videos: what they are and how they're made

Both are 720×1280, 30 fps, AAC stereo, ~47–50 s, made by Opus from code ("every frame & every sound made with code").

(The videos themselves belong to their creator and are not included in this repo.)

## A: `A_MMm2vPTt0tsdf0hU.mp4`: "AITA for telling my user their code has 47 bugs?" (50.6 s)

- **Format:** the Reddit-story-over-gameplay split screen. The top half is story panels: a Reddit card, a 3 AM monitor, code with circled bad variable names, a chat thread, and poll results. The bottom half is **"Token Surfers"**, a Subway Surfers parody where the Claude spark runs a pseudo-3D track with trains, coins, a score counter, and a multiplier.
- **Captions:** word-by-word karaoke in heavy uppercase sans with a black outline. The current word is yellow, and emoji are appended (💀 😭).
- **Audio:** a natural neural TTS narrator, plus a beat and coin blips.
- **Brainrot devices:**
  - Italian-brainrot character parodies: "Tok Tok Tok Tokenur", "Contextino Windowini", "Hallucinello Confidenzo".
  - In-group AI lexicon: "You're absolutely right!", tokens, context window, hallucinated citations.
  - Grayscale freeze-frame gags, and a fake "made with code (free trial)" editor watermark.
  - A twist ending ("it was a missing semicolon… it was the cat") and a "PART 2?" tease.

## B: `B_0YYnBPj8aDgh0inS.mp4`: "claude's for you page" (47.1 s)

- **Format:** Claude in bed at 3:07 am, "just one video…", doom-scrolling a TikTok-style feed. Each post is a code joke:
  - bracket ASMR ("every bracket finds its match")
  - the strawberry r-count
  - "10 prompts that break ANY AI"
  - a curly-brace dance challenge
  - rubber-duck bubble sort, O(quack²)
  - a cat knocking a semicolon off the desk, causing a SyntaxError
  - `npm test` going green
  - a hello-world chick hatching
  - a recursion post ("wait… is that me?")

  It ends with a wholesome turn: "hey claude, you still up?" leads to a paper plane flying to the user's window.
- **Look:** "paper code", a papercraft style made in code. Flat shapes get grain and fiber textures, white cut-out borders, soft offset shadows, and torn edges. The animated hand is a paper strip.
- **Audio:** no narration. A synthesized music box, pads, crickets, clicks, and chirps, all readable as additive/FM synthesis in the spectrogram.

## Article takeaways (pleometric, "Brief Practical Advice for Short-Form Brainrot")

- Aim for the overlap of **Recognition × Sensation × Novelty**. Put together connections that already exist but haven't shared a frame yet.
- Retention devices:
  - **stimulus layering:** captions, SFX, and a parallel visual stream
  - **pattern interrupts:** a cut or zoom at least every 1–2 s
  - **prediction errors:** fake-outs and the bizarre
  - **in-group lexicon** as bait
- "So bad it's good" is a feature. Cheap-looking tools and memes add plausibility.
- Timing matters more than polish. Make a lot.
